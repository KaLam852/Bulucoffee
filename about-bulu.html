About Bulu

Be Unique Live Unique

Our Philosophy

We are a Hong Kong-based company with an ambition to spread the Art of Coffee. Sustainable practices are at the core of Bulu Coffee's products - We research each ingredient to ensure it serves the purpose of being fairly traded and is environmentally friendly.

我們是一家以傳播咖啡藝術為目標的香港公司。可持續實踐是 Bulu Coffee 產品的核心—— 我們研究每種成分，以確保其達到公平交易和環保的目的。

We provide our services to anyone interested in creating coffee-related products and looking for a hands-on approach in exploring the unique skill of coffee-related creation.

我們為任何對咖啡相關創作感興趣並且正在尋找實踐方法的客人提供我們的服務。

Ka Lam (Founder)

With over 10 years of experience in the Coffee and Beverages arena, Ka has in-depth knowledge and well-rounded industry experience.

Her passion in coffee and beverages began during her initial years as a barista. The morning coffee lovers leaving her counter with a smile gave Ka a sense of satisfaction. She wanted to give her clients surprises so she started to venture out improving recipes and creating more unique tastes.

創辦人 Ka Lam 在咖啡和飲料領域擁有超過 10 年的經驗，擁有深厚的知識和全面的行業經驗。她對咖啡和飲料的熱情始於她作為咖啡師的最初幾年。她致力於通過獨特咖啡創作，幫助客人為他們的品牌建立一個故事。
