Our Ambition

Spreading the Art of Coffee

Bulu Coffee is a Hong Kong based company with an ambition to spread the Art of Beverages.

Sustainable practices are at the core of Bulu Coffee's products - We research each ingredient to ensure it serves the purpose of being fairly traded and is environmentally friendly.

我們是一家以傳播咖啡藝術為目標的香港公司。可持續實踐是 Bulu Coffee 產品的核心—— 我們研究每種成分，以確保其達到公平交易和環保的目的。

Ka Lam (Founder)

With over 10 years of experience in the Coffee and Beverages arena, Ka has in-depth knowledge and well-rounded industry experience.

Her passion in coffee and beverages began during her initial years as a barista. She wanted to give her clients surprises so she started to venture out improving recipes and creating more unique tastes.

創辦人 Ka Lam 在咖啡和飲料領域擁有超過 10 年的經驗，擁有深厚的知識和全面的行業經驗。她對咖啡和飲料的熱情始於她作為咖啡師的最初幾年。她開始從咖啡烘焙探索，了解咖啡豆之間的差異，並致力於通過獨特咖啡創作幫助客人為他們的品牌建立一個故事。
